How to play

  1. Read the card. A footballer appears with the clues your level allows.
  2. Pick his club. Tap the club he spent the longest stretch of his career at.
  3. Part marks count. Any club he really played for scores — but his main club scores the most.
  4. Chain them. Correct answers build a streak and a combo multiplier.
  5. Don't stall. A wrong pick costs a life and 50 points. Run out of lives or time and it's full time.
